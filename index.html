AGROCONNECT 2.001

AgroConnect 2.0

A trusted multi-role agricultural marketplace designed around the gap between farmers and buyers — including verification, selling, delivery, pricing and payment workflows.

PROBLEM

Traditional agricultural marketplaces can struggle with direct farmer–buyer connections and trust. Anyone being able to claim to be a farmer creates a verification problem for buyers.

SOLUTION

A connected marketplace where farmers, buyers, fertilizer sellers, delivery partners, verification employees and administrators interact through role-specific workflows.

KEY FEATURES
  • Multi-role authentication and authorization
  • Farmer verification before produce publishing
  • Product, cart and order management workflows
  • Fertilizer seller and delivery workflows
  • Notifications and realtime functionality with Socket.IO
  • Cloudinary media, payment-related workflows and CI/CD deployment work
TECHNOLOGY
ReactViteNode.jsExpress.jsMongoDBMongooseSocket.IOCloudinaryGitHub ActionsVercelRender
MY ROLE

Individual developer / project owner.

WHAT I LEARNED

Designing a product around connected roles means thinking beyond isolated features and making verification, authorization and operational workflows work together.
